# Millstream Hollow

Millstream.blend is the editable Blender 4.3 scene. Millstream.glb is its runtime export. The meadow, stream, flower beds, mountain silhouettes, set dressing, camera and lighting were authored for Phoenix World.

The mill, cottages, stone bridge, well, farm props, lily pads and clouds are from Kay Lousberg’s [KayKit Medieval Hexagon Pack 1.0](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Medieval-Hexagon-Pack-1.0/tree/84fa4e91af6a88989be7c99e0891cede11f2ca38), CC0. Trees, shrubs, ferns and boulders use Quaternius Stylized Nature MegaKit Standard, CC0. The trees have local foliage colour variations. Source revision and license for the nature kit are in ../art-slice/sources.json and ../art-slice/models/nature-license.txt.

Fern Scout and Lantern Phoenix are original Phoenix World characters. Travellers.blend contains both models and the packed, hand-drawn facial expression atlases. Travellers.glb contains the runtime actors and their animation pivots.
